How to request deletion

You can request deletion of your Gas Monitoring App account and all associated personal data at any time. Since account deletion isn't yet available directly inside the App, send us a request using the steps below and we'll process it manually.

  1. Email us at mmagota2@gmail.com from the address or with the phone number linked to your account.
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the phone number you registered with, so we can locate your account.
  3. We will verify the request and confirm back to you once it has been received.

We process deletion requests within 30 days of verification, and you'll receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Account details (name, email, phone number, gender, password)Yes — permanently deleted
Registered cylinder/sensor devices and their nicknamesYes — permanently deleted
Gas level readings, alert history, and usage trendsYes — permanently deleted
Wallet balance and refill/transaction historyYes, except where retention is required below
Note on financial records: where we are legally required to retain transaction records for accounting, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes, that specific transaction data may be retained in an archived, non-personally-searchable form for the legally mandated period, then deleted. This does not affect the deletion of the rest of your account or profile data.

Partial deletion

If you'd only like specific data removed (for example, unlinking a sensor device without deleting your whole account), mention that instead in your email and we'll action just that request.
