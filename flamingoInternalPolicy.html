Flamingo Internal – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · App: Flamingo Internal (iOS and Android)

In short
  • Flamingo Internal is a work app for staff of the Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF), Tanzania.
  • It collects only what staff need to do UCSAF's work: their account, the records they create, and – when they allow it – their location, camera and photos.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps, and personal data is never sold.
  • Face ID and fingerprints never leave the phone; the app only learns whether the check succeeded.

1. Who we are

Flamingo Internal ("the app") is provided by the Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF), a public body of the United Republic of Tanzania, for use by its staff and authorised officers. UCSAF is responsible for the personal data processed through the app.

2. Who the app is for

The app is for UCSAF staff with an account created by UCSAF. It is not intended for the general public or for anyone under 18.

4. Data kept on your phone

  • Your sign-in session is kept in the phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore).
  • Some records and drafts are kept in an on-device database so you can keep working without a connection. They are sent to UCSAF's servers when you are back online.
  • Settings such as language, theme and whether biometric sign-in is on are stored on the phone.
  • Signing out removes your session from the phone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

5. Who we share it with

  • UCSAF: data you enter is stored on servers run by or for UCSAF and can be seen by authorised UCSAF staff according to their role.
  • Map services: to draw maps, the app downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and may use Google Maps. These services receive your device's IP address and the map area being shown, under their own privacy policies (OpenStreetMap, Google). If you tap "directions", the Google Maps app or website opens and its own policy applies.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

6. Security

All traffic between the app and UCSAF's servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in sessions are kept in the phone's secure storage, access depends on each user's role, and the app warns when it runs on a rooted or jailbroken device.

7. How long we keep it

Work records are kept as long as UCSAF needs them for its work and as required by Tanzanian law on public records. Account details are kept while you work with UCSAF and for a reasonable period afterwards. Data kept on the phone stays there until you sign out or delete the app.

8. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 of Tanzania, you may ask to see the personal data UCSAF holds about you, have it corrected, or object to or ask for limits on its use, within what the law allows for public records. You can turn off location, camera, photos and Face ID access at any time in your phone's settings; features that need them will then stop working.

To delete your account, ask UCSAF's ICT unit. Work records may need to be kept as public records even after the account is closed.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be announced in the app.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or your personal data, contact the Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF), Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, through www.ucsaf.go.tz.
