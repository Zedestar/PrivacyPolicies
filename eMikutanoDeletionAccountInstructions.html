eMikutano Account Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your eMikutano account, please follow the instructions below. Once your account is deleted, all personal data associated with your account will be permanently removed.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the eMikutano app on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to Settings from the side menu.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Tap on Delete Account.
  5. Follow the confirmation prompts to permanently delete your account.

Need Assistance?

If you encounter any issues while deleting your account, you can contact our support team via email: support@emikutano.co.tz

Thank you for using eMikutano.
