Flamingo External – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · App: Flamingo External (iOS and Android)

In short
  • Flamingo External connects the Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF), Tanzania, with its partners: mobile network operators, service providers, sub-contractors, government entities, researchers and members of the public.
  • It collects your account details and the project and site records you send to UCSAF, plus your location, camera and files only when you choose to use them.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps, and personal data is never sold.
  • Face ID and fingerprints never leave the phone; the app only learns whether the check succeeded.

1. Who we are

Flamingo External ("the app") is provided by the Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF), a public body of the United Republic of Tanzania. UCSAF is responsible for the personal data processed through the app.

2. Who the app is for

The app is for UCSAF's partners and members of the public who work with UCSAF, and for their authorised staff. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

4. Data kept on your phone

  • Your sign-in session is kept in the phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore).
  • Self-PAT checklists and other drafts can be saved on the phone so you can work without a connection. They are sent to UCSAF's servers when you are back online and choose to sync.
  • Settings such as language, theme and whether biometric sign-in is on are stored on the phone.
  • Signing out removes your session from the phone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone, including unsent drafts.

5. Who we share it with

  • UCSAF: data you send is stored on servers run by or for UCSAF and can be seen by authorised UCSAF staff according to their role, and by authorised people in your own organisation where the project allows it.
  • SMS delivery: your mobile number is passed to the SMS service UCSAF uses, only to send your verification code.
  • Map services: to draw maps, the app downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and may use Google Maps. These services receive your device's IP address and the map area being shown, under their own privacy policies (OpenStreetMap, Google). If you tap "directions", the Google Maps app or website opens and its own policy applies.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

6. Security

All traffic between the app and UCSAF's servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in sessions are kept in the phone's secure storage, access depends on each user's role and organisation, and the app warns when it runs on a rooted or jailbroken device.

7. How long we keep it

Project and site records are kept as long as UCSAF needs them for the project and as required by Tanzanian law on public records. Account details are kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period after it is closed. Data kept on the phone stays there until you sign out or delete the app.

8. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 of Tanzania, you may ask to see the personal data UCSAF holds about you, have it corrected, or object to or ask for limits on its use, within what the law allows for public records. You can turn off location, camera, photos and Face ID access at any time in your phone's settings; features that need them will then stop working.

Deleting your account: you can ask UCSAF to delete your account and the personal data linked to it using the contact details below. Records that UCSAF must keep as public records, such as reports already submitted for a project, may be kept after the account is closed, without your personal contact details where possible.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be announced in the app.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, your personal data or deleting your account, contact the Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF), Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, through www.ucsaf.go.tz.
