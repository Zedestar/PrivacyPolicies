Privacy Policy – eBarua Email App

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information we collect

eBarua accesses and transmits:

  • Email addresses
  • Email messages and attachments (files, photos, audio, video)

2. How we use your data

To send and receive emails, display messages and attachments, and synchronize your inbox with your email provider.

3. Data sharing

We do not share your email messages, attachments, or email addresses with third parties. All data is transmitted directly between your device and your email provider.

4. Data storage

Your emails and attachments are stored on your email provider's servers, not on eBarua servers. Temporary data may be cached locally on your device.

5. Security

All data transmitted is encrypted using standard protocols (IMAPS, SMTPS, TLS/SSL).

6. User control and deletion

You can delete your account or uninstall the app at any time to remove data stored locally. Emails stored on your provider’s server are subject to their policies.
