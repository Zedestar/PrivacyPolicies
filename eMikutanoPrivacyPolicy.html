Privacy Policy - eMikutano Mobile

Effective Date: January 24, 2026

Introduction

eMikutano Mobile ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application for scheduling, joining, and managing online meetings.

Information We Collect

  • User Authentication Data: Email, password, OAuth2 tokens, and profile information for registered users.
  • Guest Meeting Data: Meeting ID and any dynamic form information entered to join a meeting.
  • Device & App Information: Device type, operating system version, app usage analytics (optional).
  • Camera Access: The app may request camera access solely to participate in meetings.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users and manage account access.
  • To allow users and guests to join and participate in meetings.
  • To provide push notifications and calendar synchronization.
  • To improve app functionality and user experience.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption of tokens and secure storage on the device. Your data is not shared with third parties without your consent, except as required for the operation of the service.

Permissions

The App requests access to your device camera to enable video meetings. Camera access is only used for meeting participation and is not stored or shared outside the app.

Third-Party Services

Our app may integrate third-party services (e.g., Jitsi) to facilitate video conferencing. These services are bound by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies as well.

Children's Privacy

eMikutano Mobile is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us
