Account Deletion Request

This page allows users of SAS — Signature Attendance Stamp to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your SAS account, please follow the steps below:

  1. Send an email request to your organization administrator or official SAS support channel.
  2. Include the following information in your request:
    • Full name
    • Registered email address or user ID
    • Organization name
    • Reason for account deletion (optional)

What Happens After Deletion

  • Your account access will be permanently disabled
  • Your personal information will be removed from active systems
  • Attendance, signature, and visitor records linked to your account will be deleted or anonymized according to organizational policies

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for legal, audit, or organizational compliance requirements. Retained data will not be used for any other purpose.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7–14 business days after verification.

Need Help?

If you have questions regarding account deletion, please contact your organization administrator or the official SAS support channel.
